## Current strongest recommendation shifts

1. Retire absolute phrases such as “nobody else builds” and “no implementation anywhere.” Use feature-, date-, threat-model-, and deployment-bounded claims.
2. Treat W4 as an interoperability compiler and assurance layer over AuthZEN, Cedar, OPA/Rego and relationship-authorization systems, not a new general policy language.
3. Narrow W6 differentiation to its exact authority-intersection algebra, multi-principal composition, proof obligations, cross-stack enforcement and receipt binding.
4. Narrow bounded-revocation differentiation to a linearizable, durable and measured end-to-end guarantee. SAGA establishes peer-reviewed open design/code prior art for execution-count budgets, but its reviewed implementation overshoots under concurrency and does not establish a release-consistency SLA.
5. Reject the standalone-greenfield SAFE premise and quarantine the current X9 OCSF exporter. Build a thin SAFE governance and assurance profile over OCSF 1.9 Incident Finding; emit loss-declared AICIE/OECD, STIX and CSAF projections; use authenticated HTTPS or TAXII plus TLP/IEP; claim interoperability only after two independent producer/consumers pass the negative corpus.
6. Define runtime AIBOM novelty as verified action-level binding to loaded weights and runtime state, not runtime inventory by itself.
7. Correct the repository's current provenance claim from SLSA Build Level 3 to Level 2. GitHub requires an appropriately isolated reusable build workflow for Level 3; an artifact attestation in the same repository-defined build job is not sufficient.
8. Treat dependency and release reproducibility as an architectural assurance property: govern all 54 Python projects, 12 Go modules, nine npm package roots and 41 Rust manifests rather than the small subset currently covered by locks, updater policy and SBOM generation.
9. Retire “nobody signs evals or pins the grader.” Current free full-text prior art includes TEE-attested audit execution, a signed evaluation-bundle design and implemented signed gateway records. Consume Every Eval Ever as the semantic baseline; differentiate on an EEE-compatible DSSE/in-toto profile with authority binding, authenticated grader/evaluator identity, measured lossless adapters, optional receiver/TEE corroboration, receipt-set reconciliation and production evidence.
10. Replace the repository's top-down TAM/SAM/SOM figures with a transparent bottom-up scenario model. The current ledger has directional adoption and ecosystem data, not evidence supporting the stated dollar ranges or first-year ARR.
11. Remove the untested 80% gross-margin assertion. Separate compute pass-through from higher-margin policy, verification, retention, support and conformance products, then measure cost per mediated action and verified evidence bundle.
12. Narrow the Qatar legal-entity statement. QCB requires provider and contracting evidence, supplier assessment and approval for high-risk arrangements; the reviewed text does not establish that every self-hosted open-source component needs its own Qatari entity.
13. Correct the EU timeline and scope. General-purpose AI obligations entered application on 2 August 2025 under transition rules, and Article 55 applies to providers of models with systemic risk; it is not a universal 2027 one-command compliance event.
14. Freeze the attestation substrate: DSSE/in-toto for portable statements, RATS/EAT/CMW for appraised measured evidence, and SCITT/COSE Receipts for optional transparency. Warrantor should own authority, enforcement conformance and authenticated expected-set reconciliation—not another generic envelope.
15. Reject the bundled Rekor v1.3.6 and SPIRE/mTLS paths as supported trust infrastructure. Replace them with pinned upstream Rekor v2 and SPIRE profiles that verify checkpoint/proof/time and credential/peer authorization separately; keep transparent registration optional and store SVID validation evidence, not only a SPIFFE ID.
16. Treat “machine checked” as a layered evidence statement, not a product adjective. SentinelAgent's bounded TLA+ run reproduced while its Python runtime still allowed unsigned output authority, expired decisions, cross-scope output laundering, empty evidence, truncated reconstruction and bearer delegation. Publish formula, bound, assumptions, distinct/generated states, checker receipt, proof-to-code mapping and deployment boundary for every claimed invariant.
18. Adopt AuthZEN 1.0 and profile COAZ rather than inventing another PDP/PEP API. Do not adopt COAZ-MCP Draft 1 unchanged: generate the method map from MCP 2026-07-28, deny unknown state notifications, pin mapping provenance, and require atomic exact-operation binding plus result/effect reconciliation.
19. Adopt Cerbos and OpenFGA as differential PDP targets, not policy-semantic equivalents. Reject both reviewed gateway paths unchanged: repair final AuthZEN fields and approval correlation, cover every supported MCP primitive/transport, freeze the authorized operation, consume one permit atomically and emit decision/forward/result/effect reconciliation evidence.
20. Retire “nothing tests an authority substrate's invariants.” Build a narrower independent corpus that follows authority inputs through decision, permit, exact wire operation and observed effect. Import AgentFuzz, ASB, AgentDojo, Chord and ToolHijacker attack families, but require blind authorship, semantic utility, adaptive/repeated attacks, mutation score, cross-engine semantics, cross-implementation transfer and signed evidence before calling the profile conformance.
21. Retire “nothing ties a successful eval attack to production denial.” F5 supplies a direct current scanner-finding→policy→stage/apply→retest counterexample; Defender, Datadog and Splunk provide mature lifecycle components; US8572750, US7895649 and EP3035637 establish older disclosure of generation/lowering/rescan, integrity/aging and policy-filtered adaptive response. Consume those substrates and build only the authenticated least-authority, target-equivalent and independently reconcilable R10 composition. Keep patent chronology separate from implementation evidence and obtain counsel before legal/FTO conclusions.
22. Retain W5's product outcome but reject its present implementation and generic eBPF build path. Current W5 is E1 decision-only and accepts unsigned/invalid/expired catalogs, arbitrary modes, caller-swapped endpoints, destination-like capabilities, metadata userinfo and attacker self-signed receipts. Adopt OpenShell as the first E3–E6 substrate, add Cloudflare or Kubernetes/Cilium as a differential target, and claim non-bypassability only after the eighty-four-vector corpus plus independent result/effect reconciliation passes.
