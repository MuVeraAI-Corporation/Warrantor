Prior art landed on three pieces while nobody was reading arXiv

The agent-security research field published against this thesis all through 2026 and the existing corpus caught none of it. AgentThread (2606.28690) formalizes agent-protocol composition in TLA+ with 35 findings across five protocols — which narrows what the enforcement-tier paper can claim. DEMM-Bench (2606.20634) benchmarks whether governance evidence is sufficient, not merely present — the exact move the receipts paper was built on.

Neither kills a piece. Both change one. The receipts paper in particular gets stronger: DEMM-Bench validates sufficiency against a property set, never against a supervisory demand — so the mapping from OCC 2026-13, the RBI June 2026 draft and SDAIA onto those properties is a real, unoccupied contribution.

⭐ A central bank mandated a kill switch and did not say what one is

Verified after the catalog was built: the RBI Draft Guidance on Regulatory Principles for Model Risk Management, 2026 (24 June 2026, comments closed 24 July) covers AI/ML across eleven categories of regulated entity, and at Chapter V-B.3 requires “override, suspension, and deactivation mechanisms — including kill-switch arrangements for AI models.” No technical specification of a kill switch is provided. It also holds an institution fully accountable for third-party models, with independent validation mandatory even where a vendor has certified them.

Two months earlier the US agencies put agentic AI out of scope. Opposite decisions, identical gap — a control that is required or expected, and specified by nobody. That comparison became B-14, and on argument strength it is the best piece in the program.

Three corrections to what was on record

RBI FREE-AI is August 2025, not 2026 — and a June 2026 RBI draft now supersedes it as the live consultation surface. USENIX Security '27 Cycle 1 closed on 25 August 2026, five days before this program was built. And the agencies have signaled an RFI on AI model risk that was not previously recorded — which turns the whole US argument from a thesis into a clock.
