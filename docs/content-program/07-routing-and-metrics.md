## Routing rules, in priority order

1. **The argument's object decides the lane, not its audience.** A board-facing piece about
   containment mechanics is Agentic Attack Surface with a Governance Layer cross-post, not the
   reverse. Audience determines the *cut*; subject determines the *home*.
2. **One canonical home per piece.** Cross-posting is syndication with canonical-back, never
   dual-publication. Per `vj-substack`, SEO and AI-citation equity must accrue to one place.
3. **A piece that fits two lanes is under-specified.** If routing is genuinely ambiguous, the thesis
   is doing two jobs — split it or sharpen it before publishing.
4. **Sovereign Stack never becomes the India lane.** Standing doctrine. If a Sovereign Stack piece
   has only one regional pillar, it is not ready.
5. **Regulatory pieces lead with US/NA, GCC or India.** The EU appears only where factually required.

# Part 2 — Quantified targets

**Baseline: near-zero inbound, starting cold.** So these are **first-instance milestones**, not
growth rates. A percentage lift on zero is meaningless; a first is not.

## Program-level, reviewed at day 45 and day 90

| # | Milestone | Day 45 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Pieces published | 4 | 10 |
| P2 | **Attribution instrumented** — you can say which piece produced which conversation | ✅ required | maintained |
| P3 | First inbound conversation traceable to a named piece | 1 | 3 |
| P4 | First third-party citation of any piece | 0 (not expected) | 1 |
| P5 | IEEE S&P submission filed | n/a | ✅ or documented deferral |
| P6 | First standards-channel contribution lodged | 0 | 1 |

**P2 is the gate.** Without attribution, every other number is a story. Instrument before publishing
the second piece — a distinct URL or UTM per piece per channel, and one question in every intro
call: *what did you read?*

## Per-piece targets

Each piece gets one primary metric, a first-instance target, and a review date. **A piece that
misses its target twice gets re-cut or retired** — that rule is the point of having targets at all.

## What these targets are not

They are **not** engagement metrics. No impressions, no likes, no follower counts. A piece that
reaches four hundred people and produces one qualified conversation has outperformed one that
reaches forty thousand and produces none — and optimizing for the second number is how a technical
authority estate turns into a content mill.

They are also deliberately **small**. From a cold start, two traceable conversations from the
strongest piece in the program is a real result. Targets that look impressive and are missed teach
nothing; targets that are hit tell you the machine works and can be scaled.

## Review discipline

**Day 45:** P1–P3 against target. Any piece that has produced nothing and had a fair run gets re-cut
or retired. **Day 90:** the full table, plus the one question that matters — *which piece produced
the best conversation, and what did it have that the others didn't?* That answer sets the next
quarter's catalog.
