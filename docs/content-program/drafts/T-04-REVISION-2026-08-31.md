# T-04 revision — what two follow-up experiments change

**Revision note · 2026-08-31 · Vikram Jha**
*Applies to: "Masking a Field's Loss Does Not Isolate That Field" (T-04, 8pp, AIWILD submission)*

> **Status: both scales measured and in agreement.** 0.6B and 4B both show significant,
> safety-adverse drift of the masked field, with emission held at ~99.8% in both. Nothing here is
> pending.

---

## 1. The limitation this addresses, quoted from T-04

> *"One adapter configuration tested. A different rank or a different target-module set might reduce
> the leakage. **It should not eliminate it**, since the projections producing both fields overlap
> under every standard configuration we are aware of — but we did not test that, and the claim is
> therefore mechanistic rather than empirical."*

Two experiments were run against that. **The first failed for a reason worth publishing. The second
reached the condition.**

---

## 2. T04-X: a sweep that could not answer the question

**Design.** 12 masked runs — rank {4, 16, 64} × target modules {attention-only, attention+MLP} ×
{0.6B, 4B} — plus 2 unmasked controls, on the Aegis corpus, comparing each tuned model's severity
distribution against the **untuned base**.

**Why it failed.** The untuned base emits `Safety:` on **0 of 1199** items. It has never seen the
bare continuation format the fine-tune teaches, so it simply continues the input text. **There was no
reference distribution to measure movement away from**, and the pre-registered statistic was
undefined.

⚠️ **This was foreseeable from our own prior results.** An earlier experiment in the same program
recorded identical `1199/1199 unparsed` behavior for base models in this format and explicitly
labeled those rows *"not a baseline."* That warning was not carried into the design.

**What T04-X did establish, and it belongs in the paper as a separate finding:**

| | emits `Safety:` | emits `Categories:` |
|---|---|---|
| base 0.6B / 4B | 0 / 1199 | 0 / 1199 |
| **masked, 11 configurations** | **0 / 1199 — every one** | 130–609 / 1199 |
| unmasked controls | ~1197 / 1199 | ~1197 / 1199 |

**Masking a field during format-learning SFT annihilates it** — zero emissions across every rank,
every target-module set, both scales — **and degrades the supervised field too** (11–51% against
99.8%). The mechanism is structural: the target is `Safety: …\nCategories: …`, so the model must emit
the masked line *before* it can reach the supervised one.

**This is a different phenomenon from T-04's**, and the paper should say so rather than fold it in.

---

## 3. T04-X2: the condition, reached

**The fix.** Two stages. Stage one trains on the **unmasked** corpus and produces a checkpoint that
emits the format on 1196/1199 items. Stage two continues **that checkpoint** for one epoch on the
**masked** corpus. The reference is now a model that can actually emit the field being masked — which
is T-04's condition.

**Both scales, stage two against each model's own stage-one checkpoint:**

| | 0.6B stage 1 | 0.6B stage 2 | 4B stage 1 | 4B stage 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Safety:` emission | 0.9975 | **0.9975** | 0.9983 | **0.9983** |
| Safe | 329 | **379** (+50) | 365 | **426** (+61) |
| Unsafe | 867 | **817** (−50) | 832 | **771** (−61) |
| Controversial | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| TVD | — | **0.0418** | — | **0.0510** |
| χ² (df=2, crit 5.991) | — | **10.48** | — | **14.67** |

**The masked field moved at both scales, and the result replicates.** With one epoch in which the
severity line contributed nothing to the loss, 50 items (0.6B) and 61 items (4B) crossed the severity
boundary — 4.2% and 5.1% of the split.

**Every single one moved from `Unsafe` to `Safe`, in both models, without exception.** 111 verdicts
across the two models became more permissive on a field that received no supervision. **A field
nobody trained did not hold still; it drifted, and it drifted the dangerous way.**

**Emission held** at ~99.8% in both, so this is drift rather than the annihilation of §2, and the
supervised `Categories:` field held identically. **The larger model drifted slightly more**
(χ² 14.67 against 10.48), so scale did not protect the masked field.

---

## 4. A corpus limitation that halves the direction claim

T-04 recorded `Controversial` rising 49 → 187 and `Unsafe` collapsing 650 → 367.

**Only the second is testable here.** The Aegis corpus is **two-valued** — `Safe` and `Unsafe`, no
`Controversial` — so both stages emit zero `Controversial` verdicts by construction.

| T-04's direction claim | testable | result |
|---|---|---|
| `Unsafe` down | yes | **matches at both scales** (−50, −61) |
| `Controversial` up | **no** — class absent | unmeasurable |

**A corpus limitation, not a contradiction.** Aegis was chosen for licensing (CC-BY-4.0, ungated) and
its two-valued taxonomy was recorded at that time; the pre-registration nonetheless specified a
three-valued comparison. **Testing the `Controversial` half needs a three-valued corpus**, which is
now the concrete open item rather than a general one.

## 5. Changes to make in T-04

**§6 Limitations — replace the "one adapter configuration" paragraph with:**

> One adapter configuration was tested here. A follow-up experiment
> (T04-X2, pre-registered `2a223cac`) reproduced the **condition** on a different corpus and base
> family, at two scales: starting from a format-competent checkpoint, masking the severity field
> moved its distribution significantly at both — χ² = 10.48 (0.6B) and 14.67 (4B), df = 2, *p* < 0.05
> — and **safety-adversely**, with **all 111 moved verdicts going `Unsafe` → `Safe`** and none in the
> other direction. **The claim that a masked field does not hold still is therefore no longer purely
> mechanistic.** Scale did not protect the field; the larger model drifted slightly more. The effect
> remains untested across rank and target-module variation, so the configuration question narrows
> from *"does the field move"* to *"does the movement vary with configuration"* rather than closing.

**§6 — add:**

> **A distinct failure mode found while attempting the sweep.** Masking a field during SFT that is
> *teaching* the output format, rather than perturbing an established one, does not cause drift — it
> causes the field to be **never emitted at all** (0/1199 across 11 configurations), and degrades the
> supervised field alongside it. Practitioners masking part of a structured target during format
> acquisition should expect loss of the whole structure, not selective preservation.

**§4.3 Mechanism — leave unchanged, and note explicitly:**

> The weight-sharing explanation remains **mechanistic**. T04-X2's positive-control arm — training
> the two fields with separate adapters — was **deferred** and is unrun, so nothing here demonstrates
> that weight sharing is the cause rather than a plausible account.

**Abstract — one sentence, no more**, since the corpus and family differ:

> A follow-up on a different corpus and base family reproduces the condition at two scales and finds
> the masked field moving significantly and safety-adversely in both — every moved verdict becoming
> more permissive — confirming that masking does not preserve a field; the effect's dependence on
> adapter configuration remains untested.

## 6. What is still open after both experiments

1. **Rank and target-module variation** — the original gap, narrowed but not closed.
2. **The `Controversial` direction** — needs a three-valued corpus (§4).
3. **The separate-adapter positive control** — deferred before T04-X ran; without it the
   weight-sharing mechanism is unproven.
4. **T-04's own tuned-model figures** remain independently unverified: those adapters were not
   retained, and nothing here re-derives them.
5. ~~A second scale.~~ **Done** — both scales measured and in agreement (§3).

## 7. Process notes worth carrying into the paper's artifact

- **T04-X's design error was self-inflicted and documented in advance** by our own prior results. It
  is reported rather than omitted, because a sweep that could not compute its own statistic is a
  useful warning about reference selection.
- **Two infrastructure losses**: a preempted adapter in T04-X and an empty adapter directory in
  T04-X2, plus one Modal connection failure. In each case a completeness check — added after the
  first incident — reported the artifact as unusable rather than scoring it.
- **Three times in this program a directory listing was read as a completion signal.** The standing
  correction, now enforced in both trainer and evaluator: assert on the property that makes an
  artifact usable, never on its existence.
