That is a finding in its own right and belongs in the paper: **the "split" verdict is robust across coders at 30 of 30; the taxonomy of why it is split is robust at only 12 of 15.**

## 1.2 Surrogate shapes

The 15 surrogates are drawn from **four** shapes, not fifteen.

| Shape | Definition | Works | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 — Per-event admissibility predicate at an application chokepoint** | A single event tuple (call, request, connection) is tested against a granted set, a compiled rule, or a presented credential. Decidable at interception from local state; default-deny in most, default-allow in one. | MiniScope, SEAgent, AARM, PAuth, the section 9 system, Separation-of-Powers, TDX trusted plane, Heartbeat-Bound Credentials, Grimlock | 9 |
| **S2 — Kernel or OS capability bound over a process tree** | Path, port, syscall, IPC or resource limits enforced below the runtime and inherited by descendants. | Sandlock, Caging the Agents, AgentCgroup | 3 |
| **S3 — Prefix-closed automaton or trace property over a plan or instruction stream** | A grammar, FSM or plan graph the executing trace must conform to; violation has a finite bad prefix. | CaMeLs Can Use Computers Too, From Craft to Kernel | 2 |
| **S4 — Single-run positive-evidence observation predicate** | A detector, not an enforcer: a witness is reported if observed; absence of a witness proves nothing. | MCP-SandboxScan | 1 |

Secondary occurrences, which reinforce rather than dilute the taxonomy: S2 appears as a conjunct inside MCP-SandboxScan (the WASI grant) and inside Caging the Agents alongside its network layer; S3 appears as a conjunct inside MiniScope (the "for every step *j*" formulation) and inside Separation-of-Powers (the typed intent stream with lineage anchor); S1 appears as a conjunct inside Sandlock (the user-notification supervisor's per-syscall Allow/Deny/Continue).

**The dominant shape is S1 at 9 of 15**, and every S1 instance shares the same four structural traits: interception before effect, a predicate over one event, evaluation against a set fixed earlier in the session, and a decision that is sound only if the event presented is the event that will occur.

## 1.3 Objective shapes

The 15 objectives are drawn from **four** shapes, and 11 of 15 contain an information-flow conjunct.

| Shape | Definition | Works where it is the lead objective | Works where it is present as a conjunct | n (lead) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **O1 — Information flow / non-interference** | Non-leakage, non-exfiltration, confidentiality, "the lethal trifecta", PHI non-disclosure, taint-to-sink non-influence. A hyperproperty over sets of executions. | MiniScope, MCP-SandboxScan, CaMeLs, AARM, Caging the Agents, From Craft to Kernel, TDX trusted plane, Sandlock, Grimlock | + SEAgent, Separation-of-Powers, PAuth (need-to-know) | 9 |
| **O2 — Intent alignment, task faithfulness, least privilege relative to an unobservable oracle set** | Whether an action serves what the user actually meant; membership in a minimal action set that is oracle-defined. | SEAgent, PAuth, Separation-of-Powers | + MiniScope, AARM, Caging the Agents, TDX trusted plane, CaMeLs | 3 |
| **O3 — Truthful provenance and accountability** | That the recorded chain corresponds to what the agents actually did. Distinct from O1: a correspondence claim about records, not a flow claim about data. | The section 9 system | + Grimlock, Separation-of-Powers, TDX trusted plane | 1 |
| **O4 — Cross-execution operational objective** | Performance isolation between co-located tenants; deterministic termination of a swarm; completion of all sessions. Not a single-trace property, and in part liveness rather than safety. | AgentCgroup, Heartbeat-Bound Credentials | — | 2 |

The modal cell is **S1 × O1** at 4 works outright. That cell is the paper's authorization-execution gap seen from the Schneider side: a predicate on one authorized event, advertised as a property of where data ends up.

**The data does not support fifteen unique pairs.** Four surrogate shapes, four objective shapes, eight occupied cells, one modal cell holding between a quarter and a half of the corpus depending on how conjuncts are counted. Naming the shapes is warranted.

## 1.5 The residual surface: where the gap lives

The gap between surrogate and objective is not undifferentiated. It resolves into **five** recurring residual shapes; every one of the 15 works falls into at least one, and most into two or three. Quotations below are verbatim from the codings, which in turn quote their source papers.

| Shape | The residual attack | Works | n | Representative coder-recorded quotation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 — In-scope misuse** | The honest, correctly authorized call that is nonetheless harmful. The predicate holds; the objective fails. | MiniScope, the section 9 system, PAuth, Grimlock, SEAgent, Caging the Agents, Sandlock, AARM | 8 | "a delegatee acting maliciously within its granted scope (e.g., calling permitted tools for harmful purposes) is out of scope for the token layer" — section 9 system. "an agent legitimately holding a narrow scope can still move arbitrary information to any peer that scope permits" — Grimlock, conservative. |
| **R2 — Unmediated path** | The effector is reached without traversing the monitor at all. The predicate is never evaluated. | MiniScope, Heartbeat-Bound Credentials, Grimlock, MCP-SandboxScan, PAuth, AARM | 6 | "any authority exercised without network egress (local file write, local process or sub-agent spawn, host-side IPC) is not an event the monitor ever sees" — Grimlock, charitable. "including local file writes, subprocess execs, egress to endpoints that never added the freshness check" — Heartbeat-Bound Credentials, conservative. |
| **R3 — Laundering through the model** | Tainted or untrusted content is re-derived by an untracked reasoning step, or a semantic decision is delegated to a probabilistic judge inside the trusted component. | From Craft to Kernel, AARM, CaMeLs, Separation-of-Powers, TDX trusted plane, PAuth | 6 | "the PPU can read tainted text and re-derive its content into a nominally untainted output" — From Craft to Kernel, conservative. "Tracking data flow is complicated when data passes through an LLM's context window, where it may be transformed, summarized, or paraphrased." — AARM. |
| **R4 — Uncomputable referent** | The objective names a set no monitor can decide membership in: the oracle-minimal action set, the user's true intent. | SEAgent, PAuth, Separation-of-Powers, AARM, MiniScope, TDX trusted plane | 6 | "T_q is oracle-defined and often not known in advance, making defense against such attacks challenging" — SEAgent. "Can we build reliable intent classifiers, or must we accept that some attacks will be semantically indistinguishable from legitimate operations?" — AARM. |
| **R5 — Cooperation dependence** | The upward half of the protocol depends on the agent volunteering true information about itself. | AgentCgroup, the section 9 system | 2 | "Declarations are advisory; the feedback loop corrects underestimates." — AgentCgroup. "AIP provides attribution binding (the signature identifies who made the claim) but does not prevent a dishonest agent from misrepresenting results" — section 9 system. |

**R1 and R2 are the two halves of the authorization-execution gap.** R1 is what remains when acceptance is bound correctly and action is unbounded within the accepted scope. R2 is what remains when the action surface is wider than the acceptance surface. Eleven of fifteen works exhibit one or both. R3 and R4 are the reasons the surrogate cannot be tightened to close R1 — the referent is not computable and the data is laundered through a component that is in the trusted set by necessity, not by argument.

## 1.6 Outliers, stated so the pattern claim survives contact

Two works do not fit the modal reading, and saying so strengthens the taxonomy rather than weakening it.

- **AgentCgroup** is the only work whose objective is not an information-flow or intent property. Its objective is operational — that co-located sessions complete — and its unenforceability is partly liveness, not hyperproperty. It is also the only work whose primary adversary placement is A1.
- **MCP-SandboxScan** is the only work whose surrogate is a detector rather than an enforcer. Its own text concedes that absence of a witness does not prove absence of a flow, which means its surrogate does not even bound the trace it observes, let alone the deployed one. It is the only work that enforces nothing at deployment time.

Both are correctly coded split. Neither is evidence against naming recurring shapes; each is evidence that the shape vocabulary has four entries rather than one.

**What each A4 work denies.** PAuth denies that *the harness's account of a call is authoritative* — the agent "may issue calls that deviate from the task for any reason", so the decision is moved out of the harness into middleware embedded in each participating server, deciding only over values arriving as signatures from other servers. Grimlock denies that *the runtime will present its actions to the mediation point at all* — it assumes "direct sockets, alternate stacks" and refuses to trust "user-layer code to correctly implement identity or authorization", so mediation is moved below the runtime into eBPF interception plus an out-of-runtime guard proxy in a separate confidential VM.

## 2.3 The mechanical reason the A2 × A4 cell does not compose

### 2.3.1 The load-bearing assumption

Placing the adversary in the inputs is not merely a statement about where attacks originate. In all ten A2 works it is also, necessarily, a statement about where the *enforcement point* lives — and in all ten the enforcement point is a component of the harness, or is fed by one:

| A2 work | Where its monitor sits | The harness component whose honesty the guarantee requires |
|---|---|---|
| MiniScope | In the agent's own process | A decorator wrapping each MCP function; the routing of calls through it |
| CaMeLs | In the harness | The plan interpreter, and the Q-VLM path that resolves runtime operands |
| SEAgent | In the framework | The System View graph, constructed from declared invocations and messages |
| From Craft to Kernel | Between the model and its effectors | The reification step producing typed instructions, and the Security Context Registry holding taint labels |
| AARM | In band, by design | Gateway / SDK instrumentation / vendor hook — all three conformant architectures are in band |
| TDX trusted plane | Split | The REE-side execution gate that extracts and normalizes the operation before the trusted plane classifies it |
| Heartbeat-Bound Credentials | At the verifier | That the agent presents a proof at every privileged action |
| Sandlock | Split | The kernel layer sits below the runtime, but the supervisor in the trusted parent answers every runtime-dependent decision |
| Caging the Agents | Layered | L1–L3 sit below the agent; L4, the prompt-integrity framework, is framework-injected metadata authored in the harness |
| MCP-SandboxScan | Outside deployment | The audit harness, and the inference that audited behavior predicts deployed behavior |

Each monitor decides over an **event vocabulary the harness supplies**: which call was made, with which arguments, against which plan, carrying which taint label, in which normalized form, bound to which identity. The soundness of every decision is conditional on that vocabulary being a faithful account of what is about to happen. That conditional is the A2 assumption, and both A4 works deny it explicitly and in writing — Sandlock most starkly of all, since its own threat model puts "the embedding agent runtime" in the trusted set while Grimlock names "a malicious or buggy agent runtime" as its adversary. The same component, in the trusted set of one work and the adversary set of the other. These are contradictory axioms, not differing emphases.

### 2.3.2 What breaks, in four steps

**(1) The A2 monitor's premises become attacker-chosen, so its guarantee is voided rather than weakened.** An A4 adversary controls the *event the monitor sees*, not merely the data the agent reads. It can present a benign call to the decorator and issue a different one; hand the interpreter a plan-conformant trace while acting off-plan; label tainted data untainted in the registry; normalize a harmful operation into a benign trusted-operation request before the trusted plane ever classifies it; construct the System View graph the Decision Engine matches against. In each case the monitor returns ALLOW *correctly*, on a false premise. The failure is not a missed deny. It is a manufactured allow, and no amount of tightening the predicate reaches it.

**(2) The A4 mechanism does not backfill the coverage the A2 work was relying on.** PAuth constrains only calls presented to a participating server. Grimlock constrains only flows crossing a sandbox boundary. Neither observes a local file write, a subprocess spawn including descendants, a package install, a sub-agent spawn, or a credential read on the host — which is precisely the remainder each A2 work left unmediated *on the strength of the harness being honest*. The composition therefore removes an assumption without adding a mechanism at the place the assumption was doing work. **The composed coverage vector is not the union of the two; it is the A4 work's vector alone**, because every cell the A2 work claimed rested on the antecedent the A4 work retracts.

**(3) The two mechanisms cannot be told apart from each other's vantage point, so the composition does not type-check.** To Grimlock, an A2 monitor co-resident with the agent is inside one opaque, attested principal: the guard sees a flow from a sandbox, not a flow that a monitor inside that sandbox approved. It cannot protect the monitor and it cannot read the monitor's verdict. To PAuth, an operand computed in the harness by an A2 mechanism — a plan-resolved coordinate, an ILP-minimized scope, a taint-cleared value, an AARM-MODIFY-rewritten parameter — carries no signed symbolic provenance and is rejected as off-slice; admitting it requires enrolling the harness as an envelope authority, which reinstates exactly the trust PAuth withdrew. Either the A2 mechanism is demoted to advisory, or the A4 mechanism is demoted to a rubber stamp. There is no third arrangement.

**(4) The composed audit trail is weaker than either component's, not stronger.** Five of the ten A2 works emit records authored at a point inside the harness: AARM's signed receipt per decision, the TDX evidence chain built from an REE-extracted request, SEAgent's System View, Caging's fleet audit, MCP-SandboxScan's witnesses. A signature attests to the record, not to the event. Under an A4 adversary these records are attacker-authored at capture even when correctly signed after capture, and a reader of the composed trail cannot tell which records crossed the A4 trust boundary and which did not. Accountability claims that hold separately do not survive the merge.

### 2.3.3 Why the corpus produced this cell rather than avoiding it

A4 appears as a **secondary** placement in 10 codings across 7 works — MiniScope, AARM (both coders), PAuth, Caging the Agents, the section 9 system, TDX trusted plane, Heartbeat-Bound Credentials (both coders). The corpus is not unaware that the harness is a threat. It declines to place it primary, because doing so would invalidate the enforcement point each work has chosen. The non-composition is therefore not an oversight in a few papers; it is the structural consequence of a shared design decision — put the monitor where the semantics are — meeting the one adversary that decision cannot survive.

## 2.5 Robustness of the 20-pair count

The count depends on two adjudications where the coders disagreed, and they disagreed in opposite directions on the same two codes:

| Work | charitable | conservative | adjudicated | effect if flipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PAuth | **A4** | A2 | **A4** | 1 A4 × 11 A2 → **11 pairs** |
| Caging the Agents | A2 | **A4** | **A2** | 3 A4 × 9 A2 → **27 pairs** |

Neither flip changes the qualitative result — the cell is non-empty and its members are non-composing for the reason given in 2.3 — but the paper should state the count as adjudicated rather than as observed, and should carry this table. Both disagreements are of the same species: a coder reading a work's *credential and runtime* discussion as a statement about the harness (A4) against a coder reading its *injection* discussion as the primary placement (A2). Where a work discusses both, the code turns on which the evaluation actually exercises.

- **The Heartbeat-Bound Credentials charitable coding stores prose rather than codes** in `adversaryPlacementSecondary`; the four entries embed A4, A3, A1 and A5 with justifications.
