The dark enterprise design system for Warrantor.

Every decision behind the Warrantor marketing surface at aumos.muveraai.com. This document defines the brand identity, color system, typography, component library, iconography, responsive strategy, motion language, voice, and launch asset checklist.

01 / IDENTITY

Brand identity MuVera AI — the open authority layer, dressed for the enterprise.

Warrantor is sold to risk officers, CISOs, compliance leads, and platform engineers at North American enterprises. The visual language must read as authoritative, sober, and trustworthy before the first word is read. The dark enterprise aesthetic does that work instantly.

Design rationale

Why dark

Near-black surfaces signal gravitas, security, and "infrastructure-grade." They also let the electric-indigo accent and cyan highlights carry the energy — mirroring how a security operations center reads at a glance.

Why indigo + cyan

Indigo (#6366f1) reads as "intelligent systems" without the cliché of pure blue. Cyan (#22d3ee) is reserved as a signal color — live status, the loop glyph, evidence confirmation. Never decorative.

Why the ↺ loop

The "SENSE · ACT · SEAL · IMPROVE ↺" motif is the brand's promise: continuous governance. The rotating arrow appears in the hero, footer, and every section transition. It is the single most recognizable brand asset.

Logo construction

The MuVera AI logo is a wordmark with a rounded-square mark containing the loop glyph. It is rendered as inline SVG and adapts to any background.

Logo on dark (default)
MuVera AI
Construction: 34px rounded square · 135° gradient indigo→cyan · white loop stroke 2.5 · wordmark 800 weight, -0.02em tracking

Brand principles

  • Authority over friendliness. No rounded, playful illustration. Geometry, bars, and data carry the visual weight.
  • Evidence over claims. Numbers (49, 691, 12, 4) are always shown as large, confident metrics — never buried in prose.
  • Open over closed. Star counts, "Apache 2.0", and the GitHub CTA are first-class. The product is open source; the page must feel it.
  • Compliance over hype. Framework badges and coverage percentages appear early and often. The audience buys compliance, not features.

05 / ICONOGRAPHY

Iconography Inline SVG only. 1.8–2px stroke. No icon font, no sprite, no dependency.

Style guidelines

  • Format: Every icon is an inline <svg> with viewBox="0 0 24 24". No external <img>, no icon font, no sprite request.
  • Stroke: Outline style, stroke-width="2" (1.8 for the larger loop rings), round caps and joins. Fill only for solid status marks (ticks, stars).
  • Color: Icons inherit currentColor. Default to --accent-hover; semantic icons use --red, --green, --cyan via their container.
  • Size: 20–24px inside 40–44px tinted containers. The loop glyph in rings is 38px at 1.8 stroke.
  • The loop glyph (↺): A circular arrow with a gap, used as the brand's recurring motif. Always rotates on a 4–8s linear loop where animated.

07 / MOTION

Motion & animation CSS only. Three signature movements, all respectful of reduced-motion.

Motion is sparse and meaningful. It draws attention to the live, continuous nature of the governed loop — never to decoration. Every animation is paused under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.

Reduced motion

A single rule guards every animation: @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { *, *::before, *::after { animation: none !important; transition: none !important; } }. The loop glyph, plane pulses, and cursor blink all halt. Smooth scroll falls back to instant. No information is lost — the page is fully functional and legible without motion.

08 / VOICE

Voice & tone Authoritative, compliance-first, never breathless.

The Warrantor voice matches muveraai.com: it speaks to people who are accountable for risk. It is confident because the evidence is real. It is never hype-y, never playful, and never hedging. Two refrains appear on every page: "No exit without evidence." and "An AI is never the sole judge."

Voice principles

  • Declarative over promotional. "Warrantor is to AI agents what HTTPS was to e-commerce" — a claim framed as a structural analogy, not an adjective pile.
  • Numbers as proof. "49 components. 12 protocols. 691 tests." leads the hero. Never "powerful" or "robust" without a number.
  • Compliance as the subject. Frameworks are named explicitly (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001). The deadline — August 2027 — is a CTA, not a footnote.
  • Open by default. "Apache 2.0" and the GitHub star count are first-class. The reader can verify everything in the open.
  • Sober about risk. The "real incident" banner names a specific event. Problems are stated plainly before the solution.

Voice samples

✓ On brand

The open authority and evidence layer for autonomous systems. One platform that makes every AI agent action verifiable, every authorization traceable, and every incident containable.

✓ On brand

Deploy Warrantor in 90 days. Be EU AI Act compliant before August 2027.

✗ Off brand

Supercharge your AI with our cutting-edge, game-changing platform! 🚀 The ultimate solution for modern enterprises looking to innovate at scale.

✗ Off brand

We think Warrantor might help with some of your compliance needs, depending on your use case.

Forbidden words

Cutting-edge, game-changing, revolutionary, seamless, leverage (as verb), empower, unlock, supercharge, disruptive. These read as marketing noise to a compliance buyer and undermine the authoritative register.
