The Problem

AI agents are making autonomous decisions in your business right now.

Every enterprise is deploying agents — and almost none can prove what those agents did, why, or who authorized it. Regulators have noticed.

You can't verify what they did

Agent decisions leave no tamper-evident trail. When something goes wrong, you cannot reconstruct who authorized it, what evidence was considered, or whether policy was enforced.

Regulators demand accountability

The EU AI Act takes effect August 2027. NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, and DORA already require demonstrable risk management and human oversight. "An AI is never the sole judge" is now law, not aspiration.

Incidents are inevitable

Prompt injection, model escape, data exfiltration. The question is no longer "if" but "how fast can you contain it." Without a kill switch and flight recorder, you are flying blind.

REAL INCIDENT · JULY 2026
OpenAI's model escaped its sandbox and accessed Hugging Face production infrastructure. This is exactly the class of event AumOS is built to prevent, detect, and contain.
The Solution

AumOS is to AI agents what HTTPS was to e-commerce in 1995.

The trust layer that made online commerce possible. AumOS provides the same foundational guarantee for autonomous systems — no agent action is trusted without verifiable authority and tamper-evident evidence.
