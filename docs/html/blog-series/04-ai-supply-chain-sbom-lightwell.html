Thesis

Pickle taught us that model files can be code. Safetensors fixed load-time execution[1,2]; conversion-service attacks showed provenance still matters[3]. AumOS supply chain is Safetensors++ + Merkle provenance + ML-BOM + Sigstore + Lightwell-class remediation—protocols P5, P6, P11.

1. Safe formats

AumOS-native extensions (not upstream standards)

S1 SafeTensors++ (proposed __provenance__ metadata) and S3 GGUF-Ext (osaf.safety block) are AumOS portfolio proposals layered on real formats (Hugging Face Safetensors; ggml GGUF). They are not upstream HF or GGUF registry standards today.

Upstream Safetensors stores tensors + JSON header without pickle opcodes[1,2]. Conversion-service attacks showed you can still poison distribution even when the format is safe[3]. Format safety ≠ supply-chain safety—you still need signatures, lineage, and admission policy.

2. AI/ML BOM

CycloneDX ML-BOM and SPDX AI/Dataset profiles are the dual standards for model+dataset inventory[4,5]. S4 ModelSBOM and P6 AATM align fields (architecture, datasets, licenses, hashes). S5 DataProvenanceKit exports signed JSON-LD lineage (model cards / datasheets culture).

3. Provenance ledgers

S2 ProvenaChain builds tamper-evident Merkle history with roots in Sigstore Rekor[6,7]. SLSA levels and in-toto steps frame training integrity for S8[9,10]. S7 TamperScan hunts weight-level backdoors and silent fine-tunes.

5. Remediation bundles vs Lightwell

IBM/Red Hat Lightwell commercializes a trusted clearinghouse for remediated open-source application dependencies—digitally signed packages, SBOMs, and coordination—not a public claim that Lightwell remediates foundation-model weights at scale[8]. That distinction is load-bearing.

S9 lightwell-bridge and P11 PRB are AumOS-proposed extensions: apply the same signed-remediation + transparency pattern to AI artifacts (adapters, skill packs, eval corpora). PRB is “VEX-inspired” status + signatures—not a claim that CSAF/VEX already defines AI weight remediation. Do not cite Lightwell as if it already ships P11.

6. AumOS map

T1 verifies artifact signatures. Skills enter the gateway only as signed P5 SSP packages (AumOS profile). Without this plane, eval scores and authority envelopes sit on untrusted weights. Implication: freeze a weight digest in AAE/VEB or you are authorizing ghosts.
