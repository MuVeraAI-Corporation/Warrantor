Thesis

Evaluation without packaging is a slide. Warrantor treats eval as evidence: orchestrate garak/PyRIT/HELM/Inspect[1–7], seal results in P8 VEB, gate CI with A4/A6, and feed independent evaluators via X6.

1. The eval tool stack

garak is breadth scanning; PyRIT is multi-turn adversarial orchestration; HELM is multi-metric baselining; AgentDojo attacks tool-using agents; Inspect is a modern harness[3–7]. A1 SafeEval YAML-orchestrates them; A2 Adversaria unifies; A5 agentsec-lab holds rotating adversarial benchmarks; A7 is continuous red-team cloud; A8 arena ranks with Elo methodology.

Figure 1 — From probes to sealed bundle

2. Verifiable Evaluation Bundles

P8 VEB packages dataset pin, harness version, model digest, scores, judge prompts, and signatures. If the bundle cannot be reproduced, the number is not evidence. Anthropic’s 141k-run retrospective[10] shows why X5 RetroSpecKit must treat transcripts as first-class forensic objects.

3. Conformance as a product

A6 is cross-language conformance for Warrantor protocols—not model safety alone. No language package ships a protocol version until vectors pass. That is how AAE/AAR avoid “JSON that kinda looks right.”

4. US / India risk anchors

US banking model-risk supervision now points to OCC Bulletin 2026-13 / Fed SR 26-2—not obsolete SR 11-7[9]. NIST AI RMF remains the general US risk framing[8]. India DPDP governs personal data in eval corpora (Essay 8). EU AI Act appears only as a passing export concern—not the spine.

5. Component map

VEB links to model digests from Essay 4 and AAE from Essay 1: you cannot claim “safe on version X” without binding weights and authority. Separate model safety eval (garak/PyRIT/HELM) from protocol conformance (A6): both produce evidence, different claim types. X5 retrospective transcript review is how you discover eval-environment escapes after the fact—pair with containment (Essay 2), do not replace it.
