Thesis

Authority and evidence must travel to the edge and into air gaps. Federated training with DP (F1/F2), edge attestation agents (F3), fleet operators (F4), CLI/console/cloud (X1/X7/X11), and sovereign bundles (X10) complete the stack—with India DPDP and US model-risk anchors where data and banks meet AI.

1. Federated + DP

F1 FedCore is attested federated training (PyTorch/NeMo class workloads). F2 DPCrate wraps differential privacy tooling (Opacus-class)[1] with budget dashboards. Flower-style orchestration patterns inform multi-party control planes[2]. Context provenance (P3) records which partitions contributed without leaking raw data.

Figure 1 — Center ↔ edge authority

2. Edge sentinel & fleet

F3 EdgeSentinel is a small Go agent for Jetson/EGX-class devices[3]—attestation heartbeats and local policy cache. F4 FleetMarshal is the K8s operator (ModelFleet CRD) for canary/blue-green[4]. Edge without CAP is just remote root.

3. Sovereign / air-gapped

X10 sovereign-stack packages air-gapped single-node deployment (Helm/Ansible patterns[5]). Offline verification modes for nvtrust and Sigstore mirrors are mandatory—sovereign does not mean “skip crypto.”

4. Cross-cutting product surfaces

X1 defstack-cli is the operator CLI for install/verify/compliance-report (Rust implementation language choice—not a claim that a CLI framework is the product). X7 console is the enterprise policy/evidence UI (Wave 7). X11 defstack-cloud is managed control. X3 OpenHarnessSpec aims at vendor-neutral harness contracts (NOOA/MCP-informed, AumOS-proposed standard). X4 CryptoAuditAI productizes AI-assisted cryptanalysis research directions[8]—with the same dual-use caution Anthropic’s work implies.

5. Regional anchors

India DPDP Act 2023 is the primary personal-data statute for Indian deployments and data residency choices in federated designs[6]. US institutions track Fed SR 26-2 / OCC 2026-13 for model-risk posture[7]. GCC sovereign AI programs care about air-gap and attestation—X10/C1-* become procurement language. EU AI Act is a secondary export concern, not the series spine.

Series closure

Essays 1–8 cover every portfolio cluster and every protocol P1–P12 via dedicated sections or the master index matrix. Authority without evidence, evidence without supply-chain binding, and multi-agent interoperability without containment are incomplete systems. AumOS is the open composition layer that refuses those incompletenesses.
