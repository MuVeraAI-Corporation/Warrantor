The master build catalog · IV: the blueprint control index

Every one of the value blueprint's 72 proposed incident-prevention controls, with its owner, horizon, W-spine link, proof gate and residual risk, indexed to the catalog items it was derived from. This is the reconciliation table between the two volumes this document supersedes: the planes A–H are a reader's index, the strata L0–L11 are the build spine, and the link in the last column is the join.

How this table was made

The 72 rows were extracted verbatim from the value blueprint's catalog data on 1 September 2026 S61 IR-11. The blueprint derived each control by re-cutting the platform OS specification's catalog onto the eight operating planes and recorded the originating item identifiers in a source field S62 §7. Those identifiers are rendered here as links into Parts 07 I–III. Every row is PROPOSED; the owner and horizon are the blueprint's proposals, not commitments, and the proof gate is what must be demonstrated before any production claim.

The blueprint's 84-capability enterprise portfolio, indexed

The value blueprint also carries an enterprise capability portfolio of 84 entries organized by system rather than by control S61. It is the operating-layer view: what an enterprise buys, configures and measures, as distinct from the incident-prevention controls above and the strata items in Parts 07 I–III. The platform OS specification's second pass diffed its catalog against this portfolio and found the twenty-two families that a single incident could not teach (Part 07 III) S62 §7.12. The portfolio actions are the blueprint's decision axis (BUILD, INTEGRATE, ADOPT, MERGE, DEFER, REJECT) and must not be read as status; Part 00 explains the two axes.
