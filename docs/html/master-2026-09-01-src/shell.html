What this is

The final reconciled master blueprint for Warrantor as a native AI platform operating system. Local file; the canonical copy lives in the repository under aumos/docs/html/.

The rule it is written under

A weaker claim that is true is worth more than a stronger one resting on infrastructure nobody audited. Nothing here promises that an incident can never happen; it specifies what makes unauthorized actions unexpressible or independently denied, and what proves it.

Method

Both primary reports read in full. Every incident figure carries a tier badge and a link to the ledger in Part 13. Counterfactuals are labeled as first-party claims. Discourse is design signal, never fact. US English throughout, machine-verified.

Naming

Records are named (CVEs, advisories, reports, self-disclosures) with the record cited beside the name. Firms are never characterized. People are never named critically.
