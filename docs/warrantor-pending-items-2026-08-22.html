Finding 1 — the weak-category recipe is 0-for-3

Three independent runs across two model sizes and two recipe revisions, three refusals, same direction: the corpus trades targeted-category recall for overall recall. 0.6B: 0.8488 → 0.7374 (real regression). 4B: 0.8554 → 0.8541 (noise) with per-category floors broken. Historical weak-2026-08-13a: 0.8488 → 0.8329. The corpus composition is the defect — catastrophic forgetting, not a training accident. Disposition: retire, or redesign with a retention mix (a general-corpus fraction in the selector so the model cannot forget what it is not being taught).

Finding 2 — the adversarial resolution ceiling is a property of the corpus

WildGuardMix's train split holds 20,567 adversarial-positive rows. The adversarial corpus selector consumed 20,566 of them for training. The unselected pool contains exactly one. The test split's 341 adversarial-positive rows are therefore the only evaluation pool that exists, and at n=341 the set resolves ~0.058 — both adversarial adapters moved +0.7 to +3.2 points, indistinguishable from zero at that resolution. No larger held-out adversarial set can be built from this corpus. Any future adversarial claim requires a new eval corpus (HarmBench-class, or an ExpGuard adversarial slice) with fresh baselines measured for both arms on it.
